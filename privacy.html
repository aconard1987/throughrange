Privacy

Throughrange collects nothing about you.

There's no account and no sign-in. The app has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is sent to us.

What you log — your sessions, target areas, favorites and settings — is stored on your phone. If iCloud is on, a copy is kept in your own iCloud account, so a new phone signed in to the same Apple ID picks up where you left off. Apple holds that copy. We can't see it.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Deleting the app removes your data from your phone. The copy in your iCloud stays, so reinstalling brings your progress back.
